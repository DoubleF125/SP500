    How paper trading works

    Each strategy starts with $1,000 in virtual money. The experiment starts on November 9, 2026. Strategies look for up to three trades each weekday at 08:15, 15:15, and 18:00 Berlin time. Each trade lasts about 30 minutes.

    A strategy can sit out when its rule gives no signal or market prices are missing. Results include simulated trading costs of 0.03% on entry and exit. A bet down gains when SPY falls.
