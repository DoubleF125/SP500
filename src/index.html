      How paper trading works

      Each strategy starts with $1,000 in virtual money. In My results, each trade uses the strategy's full remaining balance. Reset starts a new run on this browser from the next trade window. The experiment starts on November 9, 2026. Strategies look for up to four trades each weekday at 15:15, 15:45, 18:00, 21:15 Berlin time. The windows follow New York market time, so the Berlin times adjust when clocks change. Each trade lasts about 30 minutes.

      A strategy can sit out when its rule gives no signal or market prices are missing. Results include simulated trading costs of 0.03% on entry and exit. A bet down gains when SPY falls.
